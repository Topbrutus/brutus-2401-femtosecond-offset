## Falsifiability boundary

The mathematics is falsified by an algebraic, dimensional, numerical, or implementation error.

The residual-propagation statement is an algebraic consequence of the defined transformation and scale factor. It can be checked directly by high-precision arithmetic.

Any physical interpretation is a separate empirical question. A physical claim would require a defined dimensional mapping, apparatus, calibration, uncertainty budget, controls, repeated measurements, and an effect distinguishable from instrumental noise and drift.

In particular, numerical proximity or a chosen scaling operation is not sufficient evidence of a relation to the Brout-Englert-Higgs boson.

This separation between mathematical consequence and physical evidence is intentional.
