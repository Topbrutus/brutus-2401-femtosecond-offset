## Falsifiability boundary

The mathematics is falsified only by an algebraic or numerical error.

Any physical interpretation is a separate empirical question. A physical claim would require a defined apparatus, calibration, uncertainty budget, controls, repeated measurements, and an effect distinguishable from instrumental noise and drift.

This separation between mathematical consequence and physical evidence is intentional.
